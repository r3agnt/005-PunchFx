# Changelog

Newest first. Update this with every version bump in `src/version.h`.

- **0.8.1** - Slice mode B6: the **reverse half can be switched off** (kept in the code, not removed): **Shift + B6** toggles `Pitch reverse ON` / `Pitch reverse OFF` (log), default **OFF** at power-up (nothing saved). With reverse off, B6 plays the whole eighth **forward** at the raised pitch (position = phase x 2^(semitones/12), so a pitched-up eighth uses more than one eighth of the recording; at +12 the last slice runs out of recording and goes silent), restarted on every 1/8 line with a 5 ms crossfade into the audio before the slice start at the repeat. With reverse on it is the forward-then-backwards ping-pong of 0.7.2. Switching it while a slice is held restarts the slice on the next 1/8 line. Engine: `SliceBlockInfo::fx_reverse`, `Voice::rev`, `FetchInterp`. Tests: 123 checks (forward audio at +3 and -7, switching on the fly, no click at the repeat). Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.8.0** - Slice mode: (1) **the bar line is a reset.** At every bar line (the end of the 1-bar Slice loop buffer) the output restarts from beat 1, in time: a slice that is HELD is choked (it stays choked until its button is released and pressed again, so a held slice never overruns the bar), and a loop that was started by releasing a slice (carrying on from the next slice) now plays only until the bar line and then goes back to the plain loop from beat 1. A slice button pressed shortly before the bar line lands on it and plays (it is not choked); releasing a slice exactly on the bar line also goes to the plain loop. Log `Slice loop` when this happens. (2) **Pitch knob K4** (no Shift, soft takeover, MIDI CC 24): the pitch of the B6 ping-pong, whole semitones -12..+12 (power-up default +3 = knob value 0.625; turn the knob through it to take over). A change takes effect on the next 1/8 line (the held ping-pong slice restarts there, no click). Log `K4 Slice pitch (B6) - +3 st`. New: `looper::SlicePitchFromControl`, `SliceBlockInfo::fx_semitones`, `Params::slice_pitch`, `Unit::Semitones` in `param_info.h`. Tests: 118 checks in `test_slice.cpp` (old tests moved to end at the bar line, new `TestBarLineChoke`, `TestPitchKnob`). Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.7.2** - Slice mode B6 reworked again: **no pitch drop, no 1/16 retrigger**. B6 held = the held slice plays **3 semitones up** as a **forward-then-reverse "ping-pong"** and everything triggers on **1/8 lines** again (slice presses, releases and B6 itself; the 1/16 line machinery of 0.7.0/0.7.1 is gone and the slice engine is back on the 0.5.3 base). Each eighth: the first 16th plays forward through the start of the slice read at 2^(3/12) speed (like tape), the second 16th plays the same piece BACKWARDS, then it repeats every eighth. The read position moves along a triangle, so it is continuous everywhere (no seam, no crossfade at the turn or at the repeat, no stutter) and every repeat is exactly one eighth long (pitching up by reading faster would otherwise make the repeat too short or too long). B6 on/off while a slice is held restarts that slice on the next 1/8 line in the other mode; B6 with no slice held does nothing audible; a B6 held before slice mode starts is ignored until released. Constant `kSliceFxSemitones` (3) in `looper_config.h`; `kSliceFxDen`, `kSliceFxStartSemitones/StepSemitones/DropSteps` removed. Also new: **CPU meter**: the audio callback is timed with libDaisy's `CpuLoadMeter` (a couple of cycles per block) and the main loop prints `CPU avg 12% max 31% (30 s)` to the serial log every 30 s (max = the worst block in the period; 100% would be a dropout). Tests: slice 104 checks (12 new: exact ping-pong audio per eighth, B6 on/off while held, B6 before slice mode, clicks). Beat repeat B6 unchanged. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.7.1** - (superseded by 0.7.2) Slice mode B6 pitch is now **+3 semitones on the first hit, then one semitone lower on every 1/16 retrigger, 8 steps down to -5, then it stays there** (was: normal, then down to -12). The slice mode no longer uses the pitch-shifter: the slice voice itself is read faster or slower like tape (linear interpolation, `rate = 2^(semitones/12)`, starts in the pre-roll so the crossfade ends on the 1/16 line), because the slice is a recording. That is exact in pitch (up and down), has no delay (the hit stays exactly on the beat, which a delay-line shifter cannot do for a pitch UP) and no grainy splices; the second `PitchFx` (`slice_pitch_fx`) was removed again. Constants `kSliceFxStartSemitones` (+3), `kSliceFxStepSemitones` (1), `kSliceFxDropSteps` (8) in `looper_config.h`. The count restarts when a new slice is pressed and when B6 comes on; after B6 goes off the slice returns to normal speed on the next eighth line. `SliceLoopBuffer::Semitones()` reports the pitch (tests check +3, +2 ... -5 per retrigger and the exact tape-style audio). The beat repeat B6 is unchanged. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.7.0** - (superseded by 0.7.2) B6 pitch fx in **slice mode** (second FX target). With B6 held in slice mode the held slice **retriggers on every 1/16 line** (only the first half of the eighth is heard each time, restarting from its beginning) and each retrigger is **one semitone lower** than the last (first play normal, down to -12), using a second `PitchFx` (`slice_pitch_fx`) on the slice output. While B6 is on, slice presses and releases also land on **1/16 lines** (everything stays on 1/8 lines without B6). B6 itself switches on/off at the next 1/16 line (tap = one line). B6 off while a slice is held: retriggers stop and the slice goes back to repeating every eighth, re-synced to the next eighth line. Releasing the last slice button on an odd 1/16 line carries on with the second half of the playing slice, so the loop stays in time (on an eighth line it carries on from the next slice as in 0.5.3). The slice engine now looks for lines every 1/16 (`kSliceFxDen`); `SliceLoopBuffer` got `pitch_held` (in `SliceBlockInfo`), `PitchOn()`, `RepeatIndex()`, `Repeating()`, `JumpStartIndex()`, events `PitchOn`/`PitchOff` (log `Pitch fx ON (retrig 1/16)` / `Pitch fx OFF`); B6 does nothing unless slice mode is on. LED 6 shows the slice engine's B6 state in slice mode. `PitchFx` buffer 4096 -> 1024 frames (8 KB each, enough for the 10 ms lag limit) so two instances fit (DTCM 67%). Tests: 24 new checks in `test_slice.cpp` (116): odd-line slice press, retrigger every 1/16 for 3 and 4 lines, repeat/jump index per retrigger, release on odd and eighth lines, B6 off while held (eighth grid back), B6 without slice mode, clicks with B6. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.6.2** - B6 pitch fx: slow repeats (1/4 and longer, held for many repeats) drifted out of time. Cause: within a repeat the head keeps falling behind, 0.6.1 let it lag up to 32 ms and each hop only recovered 21 ms, so a long, deeply lowered repeat sat 10-32 ms late. Now a head that is more than 10 ms behind (`kPitchMaxDelayFrames` 1536 -> 480) hops forward back to about 1 frame behind (`kPitchHopFrames` 1024 -> 480) with a 5 ms crossfade (was 10 ms), so the lag stays under about 12 ms (10 ms + the drift during the hop crossfade). Price: more, smaller splices in slow repeats with a big drop (one every 10 ms / r, e.g. every 20 ms at -12), so a slightly grainier tone there, and a pure tone is detuned by up to about 25 Hz at the deepest drop (music is not). Because skipping is how the length is kept, a very short sound (a click) can fall in a skipped part. Fast repeats are unchanged. New test `TestSlowRepeatStaysInTime` (1/4 bar, steps 2, 5, 8, 12). The old values are in the comment above the constants if you prefer smoother and later. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.6.1** - B6 pitch fx tightened (it sounded flammy): the pitch shifter in `pitch_fx.h` was rewritten. Before, the shifted sound always lagged the dry sound by about 20 ms (two read heads sweeping through a 43 ms window). Now every repeat of the held slice starts a NEW read head only 1 sample behind the live signal (5 ms crossfade, in step with the repeat's own loop crossfade, the head stands still until the beat), and the head then falls behind slowly during the repeat (that is what lowers the pitch), so the hit at the start of each repeat lands on the beat at full level (tested to within 3 samples) and the first shifted repeat joins the dry one with no jump. A head that falls more than 32 ms behind in a very long repeat hops forward 21 ms (10 ms crossfade). `BeatRepeat` now reports `RepeatIndex()` (repeat number counted from the start of the loop crossfade) and `JumpStartIndex()` (sample in the block where it starts), which `main.cpp` passes to `PitchFx::Process`. Constants: `kPitchWindowFrames` replaced by `kPitchMaxDelayFrames` (1536) and `kPitchHopFrames` (1024) in `looper_config.h`. New tests: tight-attack test in `test_pitch.cpp` (34 checks), jump-index checks in `test_beat.cpp` (236). Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.6.0** - First FX: **B6 pitch-down on the beat repeat**. Hold B6 (on and off both quantised to the 1/16 line, like the stutter buttons; a short tap still gives one 1/16): each repeat of the held slice is one semitone lower than the one before (1st repeat normal, 2nd -1, 3rd -2 ... down to -12, `kPitchMaxSteps`), and it starts again from normal on a new slice, a division change or when the stutter is released. B6 with no stutter held is "armed": the next stutter steps down from its first repeat. The pitch change keeps the slice length (the repeat stays on the grid) using a new delay-line pitch shifter, `src/engine/pitch_fx.h` (two crossfaded read heads, window `kPitchWindowFrames` = 2048 = 43 ms, about 20 ms delay and a slightly grainy tone on the shifted sound; 5 ms crossfade dry <-> shifted). DaisySP's `PitchShifter` was not used: its downward shifts are inexact (-5 comes out near -7, -12 is silence) and it only accepts whole semitones. Engine: `BeatRepeat` got `pitch_held` (in `BeatBlockInfo`), `PitchOn()`, `LoopCount()`, `Repeating()`, events `PitchOn`/`PitchOff`; B6 pressed while Shift is held, or while the transport is stopped, or in slice mode, is ignored. Log `Pitch fx ON/OFF`. LED 6: fast flash until its line, then on. Tests: `test_pitch.cpp` (29 checks: exact pitch per step, bypass, no clicks, level) and 13 new checks in `test_beat.cpp`. Untested on hardware: CPU load and how the shifted repeat sounds.
- **0.5.3** - Slice release fix: after the last slice button is released the loop now carries on from the slice AFTER the one you played (slice 3 held/tapped -> 4, 5 ... 8, 1, 2 ...). In 0.5.1/0.5.2 it restarted on the same slice, so every slice sounded twice (once held, once as the start of the loop), even on a quick tap. Tests updated. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.5.2** - Slice mode now captures ONE BAR on a BAR line (`kSliceBanks` = 1 in `looper_config.h`; set it to 2 to get the old 2-bar window with two banks back). B7 ON waits for the next bar line (not the next 1/8 line) and freezes the whole bar that just ended, so **B9 = beat 1** of that bar ... B16 = its last eighth (before, the 2-bar window ended on whatever eighth you pressed on, so B9 was not the downbeat). If a whole bar has not been recorded yet (just after power-up/Start, or within a bar of leaving slice mode) B7 stays pending (LED 7 flashes fast) and switches at the first bar line where a full bar is available, so it no longer plays silence. B7 OFF, slice presses and releases are still quantised to 1/8. The loop is re-aligned to the clock every bar (every window length). Shift + B7 now does nothing and logs `Bank ignored - 1 bar only`. Tests updated: new `BarLine` helper, bank test rewritten, unfreeze test now checks the wait (92 checks). Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.5.1** - Slice mode release behaviour changed: when the last slice button is released, instead of going back to the full 2-bar Slice loop buffer from its start, the whole 2 bars play as a loop **starting at the slice you just triggered** (slice 3, 4 ... 16, then wraps round to 1, 2 ...), starting at the next 1/8 line, until a new slice is pressed or B7 ends slice mode. The loop keeps the rotation and is re-aligned to the clock every 2 bars. The seam crossfade moves to the point where the window wraps from its end to its start. Bank switches don't change a running loop. Log text unchanged (`Slice loop`). Tests updated (`PlaysRotated`) plus a new test for a second slice during the rotated loop. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.5.0** - Slice mode on **B7** (new file `src/engine/slice_loop_buffer.h`): B7 freezes the last 2 bars of the live input at the next 1/8 line and plays them as a loop (the **Slice loop buffer**, in time with the clock, re-aligned every 2 bars), replacing what you hear. The 2 bars are 16 eighth-note slices in 2 banks of 8; **buttons 9-16** play the slices of the current bank (repeat while held, newest wins, fall back to an older held button, release returns to the full Slice loop buffer in time); **Shift + B7** switches bank (starts on bank 1 = the older bar); B7 again returns to the Quantized loop buffer / live input. Everything (switch, bank, presses, releases) is quantised to the 1/8 line with 5 ms crossfades ending on the line (`kSliceQuantizeDen = 8`). The Slice loop buffer keeps its own 2-bar recording (+1.4 MB SDRAM, 10.4 MB total), so the beat repeat history is untouched; while slice mode is on, buttons 9-16 do not stutter and the latch is off. The slice mode output is fed to the Quantized loop buffer's recording input (slices are recorded, like the beat repeat) and is also mixed over its output (heard over a playing loop). Transport Stop ends slice mode at once. Naming: `loop_engine.h` / `LoopEngine` renamed to `quantized_loop_buffer.h` / `QuantizedLoopBuffer` (global `quantized_loop`; test file `test_quantized_loop_buffer.cpp`). LEDs: LED 7 = mode (bank 2 pulses, fast flash while waiting), LEDs 9-16 = slices (dim), repeating one brighter. Log: `Slice mode ON/OFF`, `Slice bank 2`, `Slice 3`, `Slice loop`. New Mac tests `test/test_slice.cpp` (84 checks). Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.4.5** - Beat repeat presses and releases are quantised to a 1/16-bar grid (`kQuantizeDen = 16` in `looper_config.h`), so a slightly early or late finger lands in time. A press waits for the next 1/16 line, then repeats the last slice-length of audio ending on that line (slice length is still the button's own division; the slice start is no longer on the division's own grid). A release also waits for the next line. A tap shorter than the wait still repeats for one 1/16. Latch (Shift + B9-B16) and flipping between held buttons are quantised the same way; Transport Stop is still immediate. Flipping to a stored slice now works out its position from where it was captured (exact, in time). Log: `Stutter 1/4 waiting` now appears on every press. Tests: `GridPosition` removed, new `TestQuantisedPressRelease`, `TestTap`, `TestHeldPressBlocksLateRelease`, old timings moved to the 1/16 lines; 214 checks in `test_beat`. Not yet heard on hardware.
- **0.4.4** - Possible fix for a glitch heard on the first pass of the 1 bar and 1/2 divisions (later repeats were clean): `kCopyChunkFrames` (frames copied from the history ring into a division's slot per audio block) lowered from 2048 to 256. Suspected cause: the copy ran only during the first pass, took longer for longer slices, and 2048 frames of SDRAM copying per block probably overran the 1 ms audio block on the Seed. Not confirmed on hardware; the Mac tests (which cannot show CPU load) are unchanged and pass.
- **0.4.3** - Beat repeat latch: Shift + B9-B16 toggles that division on/off so it keeps repeating after release (one latch at a time; Shift + another button moves it; a normal held press overrides it while down; cleared when the transport stops). Log: `Stutter latch 1/4` / `Stutter latch OFF`. LEDs: a held stutter glows at the loop-length LED level (`kLedCycleDim`, 0.02), a latched one brighter (0.15). Shift + B16 long press still prints the version (it also toggles the 1/64 latch on the press). Logic is in `main.cpp` (engine unchanged, so no new Mac test).
- **0.4.2** - Minimum tempo changed from 40 to **130 BPM** (`kMinBpm` in `looper_config.h`, fixed constant, no control; decided because only fast styles are played). All buffers shrink 3.25x: loop 5.7 MB, history 1.4 MB, stutter slots 1.8 MB, SDRAM total **8.9 MB** (was 28.7 MB). Below 130 BPM an 8-bar loop is refused (`B1 ignored - tempo too slow`) and the long stutter divisions are refused with a new message `Stutter ignored - tempo too slow` (before they would have said "too short"). New section "Minimum tempo and memory" in `docs/DESIGN_LOOPER.md` (table of memory by minimum tempo, how to change it). Tests moved to 138.89 BPM (tick = 864 samples) and 100/150 BPM for the loop refusal test; new `TestTooSlow`; 11809 / 72 / 158 checks.
- **0.4.1** - Beat repeat changes: (1) new 3/8-bar (dotted quarter, 1.5 beats) division, so the stutter buttons are now B9-B16 = 1 bar, 1/2, 3/8, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64; its grid runs on across bar lines (lines up every 3 bars). (2) Holding several buttons: each division keeps its own stored slice (captured the first time it is used in this hold); releasing the newest button, or pressing a division again, switches at once to that division's ORIGINAL stored slice, in time with the grid, with no new capture. Everything is forgotten when all buttons are released. (3) Version print moved to Shift + B16 long press; a stutter button pressed while Shift is held is ignored. One stored slice per division (SDRAM 28.7 MB total). `ClockTracker::BarAtBlockStart` added. Tests: 153 checks in `test_beat` (flip tests at every position in the slice, 3/8 over bar lines, 1-bar slice recalled after the history was overwritten).
- **0.4.0** - Beat repeat (`src/engine/beat_repeat.h`): buttons 9-15 = 1 bar, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64, held, quantised to the slice grid, repeating the slice that just finished with crossfades that end on the boundary (first hit at full level); 2-bar history + frozen slice copy in SDRAM (25.4 MB total); immediate release; most-recent-button wins with fallback; ignored when stopped or too short. Input offset on Shift+K3 (+/-20 ms, no pick-up, CC 23) moves the slice grid against the clock. The loop records the stutter output. LEDs: button flashes while waiting, glows while repeating. New Mac tests `test/test_beat.cpp` (68 checks). `ClockTracker::PositionAtBlockStart` added.
